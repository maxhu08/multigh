Details:
    With no argument, print the selected GitHub account and effective commit
    email under Identity when the welcome is enabled. Otherwise, print nothing.
    on and off save the preference for future shells without switching accounts.

    Add mgh welcome to your shell's startup greeting to display it automatically.
    It reads the locally selected account for fast startup; use mgh status for
    live authentication information. The preference is stored outside the repo.

Examples:
    mgh welcome on
    mgh welcome
    mgh welcome off
